Lead Gen, Outbound / Research

Researched, personalized outbound and lead gen, run by a team of agents.

ComplexityMedium
  • B2B sales teams
  • Agencies & consultancies
  • SaaS
  • Recruiting
Book a Scoping Call
Overview

A team of agents runs prospecting from start to finish. They find accounts that match your ideal customer, research each one across multiple models and live sources (filings, news, hiring signals, tech stack), draft a personalized first touch for email or social, and route replies into your rep's calendar and CRM. Your team spends its time on conversations, not research.

Deliverables

  • Multi‑model research agent with per‑lead briefs

  • Reply classifier & sentiment routing
  • Lead alerts in Slack or Discord

Workflow Automation

Multi‑model workflows and agents across the tools you already pay for, owned by you.

ComplexityMediumdepends on domain‑specific knowledge
  • Ops teams
  • Agencies
  • Founders
  • Finance & admin
Book a Scoping Call
Overview

End‑to‑end automation that orchestrates multiple models and nodes. n8n runs the flow, models like Claude, GPT and Grok handle the reasoning, and agent runtimes such as Claude Code, Hermes Agent and Grok Bot take on the longer, multi‑step jobs. We wire Google Workspace, your CRM and your team chat (Slack, Discord, WhatsApp or whichever app we settle on in the build) into resilient flows with retries, alerts, approvals and dashboards your team trusts. Already on Zapier or Make? We work there too.

Best forTeams copy‑pasting between Sheets, inboxes, chat and the CRM

OwnershipFull source and credentials, hosted in your own cloud
Deliverables

  • Self‑hosted n8n with multi‑model routing
  • Agent runtimes for long, multi‑step jobs
  • Slack, Discord or WhatsApp control & approvals
